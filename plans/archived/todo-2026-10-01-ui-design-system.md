# Shared controls and design-system acceptance

Historical record filed on 2026-10-01. The archived entry described an established control foundation and pending visual work, without recording a final acceptance result.

The foundation used official shadcn/Radix controls, semantic theme tokens, two shared surface treatments, Lucide icons, and Motion. New business views were expected to compose these controls rather than choose their own colors and geometry.

Pending work at the time included searchable selection, readable text over dark native material, complete dark content themes, and font preferences. The historical font proposal described separate UI/editor/Markdown choices and should not be mistaken for today's settings.

Keyboard operation, long names, narrow windows, and visual states required user acceptance. Typechecks and builds supported implementation; they did not establish appearance.
